BOLTIV STROWALLET PAYOUT CLEANUP

This deployment removes the remaining Payscribe payout integration and Payscribe environment variables/documentation.

Current Strowallet provider paths:
- Customer wallet funding: Strowallet personal virtual accounts + webhook
- Revenue wallet withdrawal: Strowallet bank list + account verification + bank transfer

Payscribe payout endpoints/functions/routes have been removed.

Note: Paystack and Flutterwave integrations used by unrelated legacy/payment flows were intentionally retained to avoid breaking those flows. They are not used by the Strowallet revenue withdrawal endpoints.
