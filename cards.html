About the BOLTIV Card

How it works
  1. Verify your identity. A one-time check using your BVN, a valid government ID and two photos, so we can issue a card in your name.
  2. Create your card. Choose Visa or Mastercard and the amount in US dollars to load. You then pay the exact naira amount by bank transfer to a secure account number generated for your request.
  3. Fund it whenever you need. Top up the same way, by bank transfer. Your card is credited automatically once the payment is received.
  4. Spend online. Use it wherever Visa or Mastercard is accepted online in USD. You can freeze or unfreeze it at any time.
Pricing
  • You pay the card amount plus a one-time card creation fee and a small payment processing fee, converted to naira at the BOLTIV rate shown before you confirm. The exact naira total is always displayed first.
  • Funding your card carries no additional card fee.
Important to know
  • Each customer can hold one card.
  • BOLTIV Cards are contactless, so they can be added to Apple Pay or Google Pay where your phone and country support it.
  • Your card’s billing address is the address you entered during verification. Some stores check it, so type it exactly as shown under Details.
  • The card is intended for online payments, mainly with US merchants. Some categories, including money transfer, cryptocurrency, betting and gambling, dating and adult services, are not supported and may be declined.
  • Payments declined for insufficient funds may attract a fee, and a card is closed after repeated insufficient-funds declines. Please keep your card funded.
  • Payments in a currency other than USD may attract additional charges.
Security
  • Your card number and CVV are shown only after you confirm your Transaction PIN, are hidden automatically after a short time, and are never stored by BOLTIV.
  • If a card request cannot be completed after your payment, the amount is refunded to your BOLTIV wallet automatically.
