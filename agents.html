Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about becoming a BOLTIV Agent.

How do I become a BOLTIV Agent?

Create or log into your existing BOLTIV account, choose "Become an Agent" from your profile, make sure your wallet has at least ₦10,000, set up your dedicated BOLTIV funding account (which requires NIN or BVN verification), and your Agent account activates once BOLTIV confirms eligibility.

Do I need a new account to become a BOLTIV Agent?

No. Your existing BOLTIV account, wallet, login and transaction history stay the same — becoming an Agent upgrades your existing account rather than creating a new one.

Is the ₦10,000 an activation fee?

No. The ₦10,000 is working capital that stays in your wallet and can be used for transactions — it is not a fee BOLTIV keeps.

Why does BOLTIV require identity verification?

BOLTIV requires a verified static funding account, backed by NIN or BVN verification, for every Agent — this keeps the platform secure and makes funding easy to reconcile.

How does BOLTIV Agent pricing work?

BOLTIV gives Agents a discounted wholesale price. You decide what to charge your own customer and keep the difference as profit, subject to applicable BOLTIV pricing rules.

Which services can I sell as a BOLTIV Agent?

Airtime, data, electricity, cable TV and other digital services BOLTIV supports and enables for your Agent account.
