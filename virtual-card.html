The BOLTIV Card is designed for online payments, mainly with US merchants. It cannot be used for Apple Account payments (App Store, iCloud+) on an account set to Nigeria, or with Apple Pay. Some categories, including money transfer, cryptocurrency, betting and gambling, dating and adult services, are not supported and may be declined. Payments declined for insufficient funds may attract a fee, and a card is closed after repeated insufficient-funds declines, so please keep your card funded. Payments in a currency other than USD may attract additional charges. Card availability may depend on verification and on the service being enabled by BOLTIV.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the BOLTIV Card.

What is the BOLTIV Card?

It is a USD virtual card you create inside your BOLTIV account. You fund it by bank transfer and use it for online payments wherever Visa or Mastercard is accepted.

Is it a physical card?

No. It is a virtual card. You can view the details inside your BOLTIV account after confirming your Transaction PIN.

Which currency does it use?

US dollars. You pay for the card and its top-ups in naira by bank transfer, and the card is loaded in USD.

Do I need to be verified?

Yes. Card issuance requires a one-time identity check using your BVN, a valid government ID and photos.

How do I fund my card?

Open the Cards tab in your BOLTIV account, choose Fund, enter the dollar amount and confirm with your Transaction PIN. You will get a one-time account number to pay the exact naira amount, and your card is funded automatically once the payment is received.

Can I have more than one card?

No. Each BOLTIV customer can hold one card.

What if my card request fails?

If a card cannot be created or funded after you have paid, the amount is refunded to your BOLTIV wallet automatically.

Can I use the card for betting, crypto or money transfers?

No. Those merchant categories are not supported and payments may be declined.

Does BOLTIV store my card number and CVV?

No. Card details are shown only after you confirm your Transaction PIN, are hidden automatically after a short time, and are not stored by BOLTIV.

Can I use it for iCloud+, the App Store or other Apple services?

Not on an Apple Account set to Nigeria. Apple currently does not accept bank cards for the Nigerian store, so the card cannot be added there as a payment method.

Can I add the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay?

Not at the moment.
