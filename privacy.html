Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

BOLTIV is a business technology ecosystem that brings Business Manager, BOLTIV Link, BOLTIV Card, Smart Features and everyday digital services together under one account and wallet. BOLTIV respects the privacy of its customers and is committed to protecting the personal information provided when using our platform. This policy explains what we collect, why, and who we may share it with.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the products and services you use, BOLTIV may collect: account information such as your name, email address, phone number and login details; wallet and transaction details, including purchases, funding and receipts; login and security activity, such as when and where your account was accessed; identity verification information, including your BVN or NIN, a valid government ID and photos, where verification is required (for example, to issue a BOLTIV Card or activate an Agent account); business information you enter in Business Manager, such as sales, stock, expenses, suppliers, invoices, team members and branches; and website content and customer information collected through BOLTIV Link, such as orders, bookings, enquiries and customer lists.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage your account, provide the BOLTIV products you choose, process transactions and subscriptions, verify identity where required, send service and security notifications, keep transaction and activity records, prevent fraud and misuse, provide customer support and improve our products and services.

4. Your Business and Your Customers

If you use Business Manager or BOLTIV Link, you may store information about your own customers, suppliers and team members. This information belongs to you, and BOLTIV handles it only to provide the tools you use. You are responsible for having the right to collect and use it, for using it lawfully, and for respecting your customers' preferences, including marketing choices. In BOLTIV Link, only customers who agree to receive offers appear in your marketing list, and you can export your customer list at any time. Business Manager records the activity of team members you add so that you can see who did what.

5. Identity Verification and BOLTIV Card

Some products require identity verification before they can be used. For BOLTIV Card, a one-time identity check using your BVN, a valid government ID and photos is required, and this information is shared with the card issuer and identity-verification providers to complete the check. Agent accounts require NIN or BVN verification for a dedicated funding account. Card details are shown only after you confirm your Transaction PIN and are not stored by BOLTIV.

6. Payments

Payments made through supported payment channels are processed through authorized payment providers. BOLTIV does not request or store your payment card PIN.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect customer information and transaction records from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. These include email verification for new accounts, a Transaction PIN for supported actions, login history, and, in Business Manager, role-based access and activity logs. You are responsible for keeping your login details and Transaction PIN private.

8. Third-Party Services

BOLTIV works with third-party providers to deliver its products and services, including service providers, payment providers, card issuers, identity-verification providers and messaging providers. Information necessary to complete a requested transaction, verification or message may be shared with the relevant provider. Those providers handle information under their own terms and policies.

9. Your Information

Customers should provide accurate information when creating an account or making transactions. You can review and update much of your account information in your profile. You may contact BOLTIV if you have questions about information associated with your account, or to ask about correcting or removing it.

10. Changes to This Policy

BOLTIV may update this policy when necessary, and the date at the top will show when it was last changed. Continued use of the platform after an update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
