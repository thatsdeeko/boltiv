ⓘHOW AUTOPAY WORKSUnderstand how your scheduled payments work⌄

AutoPay automatically purchases your selected Airtime, Data or Cable TV service on your chosen schedule using your BOLTIV wallet. You can also schedule a one-time payment for any date and time you choose.

01
Choose what you want to automate

Select the service, plan or amount and choose how often you want it purchased.

02
Set your maximum payment

Prices and availability can change. Your maximum payment tells BOLTIV the most you're comfortable paying for each scheduled purchase.

03
We check before every payment

When your AutoPay is due, BOLTIV checks the current price and availability of your selected service.

04
You're protected from unexpected charges

If the current price is within your limit, the purchase proceeds normally. If it is above your limit, we won't charge your wallet and we'll notify you.

05
Temporary provider or network issues

If a temporary issue prevents the purchase, BOLTIV may retry according to the AutoPay rules. Your approved payment limit still applies.

06
You're always in control

You can edit, pause, resume or cancel your AutoPay at any time.
