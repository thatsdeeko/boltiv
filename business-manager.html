Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the BOLTIV Business Manager.

What is the BOLTIV Business Manager?

It is a set of business tools inside your BOLTIV account. You can sell, manage stock, send invoices and receipts, keep track of customers, expenses and suppliers, manage your team and branches, and view reports.

Do I need a BOLTIV account?

Yes. The Business Manager is part of your BOLTIV account. Create an account or sign in, then open Business Manager from your dashboard.

How do I pay for it?

You choose a plan and pay from your BOLTIV wallet. The exact amount is shown before you confirm.

Can I send a receipt or invoice on WhatsApp?

Yes. On your phone, tap Share on WhatsApp and the receipt or invoice is sent as a picture. On a computer, the picture is saved so you can attach it to a WhatsApp chat.

Can I print receipts?

Yes. You can print receipts on 58mm or 80mm thermal printers, and print invoices or save them as a PDF.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. You can add team members and give each one a role, so they only see and do what you allow.

Can I run more than one branch?

Yes, as long as your plan includes it. Each plan sets how many branches and team members you can have.

What happens if my plan expires?

You are shown how many days you have left, and there is a short grace period after expiry. Renew from your BOLTIV wallet to continue as normal.
