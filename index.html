Questions, answered

What is BOLTIV?

BOLTIV is a digital services and payments platform that brings airtime, data, electricity, cable TV and exam PINs into one account.

How do I get started?

Create a BOLTIV account, complete the account setup, fund your wallet and pick a service.

How does the cashback work?

Data purchases earn cashback into a separate bonus balance. You can spend it on any service, and it expires after 90 days.

Is BOLTIV secure?

Your account is protected with sign-in authentication and a transaction PIN for purchases.

What if I need help?

Visit the contact page and we will help with your account or any transaction.
