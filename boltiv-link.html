Frequently asked questions

How much does BOLTIV Link cost?

₦5,000 for 30 days of access, paid from your BOLTIV wallet. You get one website name, such as deekostore.boltiv.ng.

What happens after 30 days?

Your website goes offline and shows a short 'temporarily unavailable' page to visitors. Nothing is deleted. Renew for ₦5,000 and it comes back online straight away.

If I renew early, do I lose the days I have left?

No. Renewing adds 30 days on top of the time you still have.

Can I keep my website name?

Yes. Once you register a name it is reserved for you permanently, even if your plan expires. Nobody else can take it, and names cannot be deleted.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You pick a layout, colours and font, upload your logo and photos, add your products and services, and see a live preview as you go.

Can customers order from my website?

Yes. Every product has an Order on WhatsApp button that opens a chat with you and the product already filled in.

Can I see how many people visit?

Yes. Your dashboard shows page views, visitors, WhatsApp, order and call taps, and where visitors came from.

Can I delete my website?

No. Websites and names cannot be deleted. This protects your name and your content. If your plan ends, your website simply goes offline until you renew.

Can I edit my website while it is offline?

Yes. You can keep editing your content. You need an active plan to publish changes.

How many photos can I add?

Up to 24 photos in your gallery, plus photos for your products, services, logo, cover and background. Photos are made smaller automatically so your site stays fast.

Which names can I choose?

3 to 30 letters, numbers and hyphens. A few names such as www, admin and api are kept for BOLTIV.

Can I use my own .com or .ng domain?

Not yet. Every website uses a boltiv.ng address for now.
