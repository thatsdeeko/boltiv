Frequently asked questions

What is BOLTIV?

BOLTIV is a business technology ecosystem for Nigeria. It combines Business Manager, BOLTIV Link, the BOLTIV Card and Smart Features under one account and one wallet, so you can start, operate, manage and grow a business from one place.

Who is BOLTIV for?

Individuals who want simple everyday payments, entrepreneurs starting out, SMEs that need to organise sales, stock and teams, and established businesses that need control across branches.

Do I need to use every product?

No. Each product works on its own. Start with the one you need now and add others later without opening a new account.

How do I pay for BOLTIV products?

You fund your BOLTIV wallet with an available payment method and pay for supported products and subscriptions from the balance. You always see the price before you confirm.

Can I upgrade as my business grows?

Yes. Business Manager has Starter, Growth and Enterprise plans that differ by branches, team members and features, and you can move between plans from your account.

Where can I get help?

Visit the contact page to reach the BOLTIV support team by email or phone.
