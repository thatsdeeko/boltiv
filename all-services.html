Buy airtime and data for MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile

To recharge a phone on BOLTIV, enter the phone number, choose the network and pick an amount. Quick options run from ₦100 to ₦5,000, or you can type any amount. Payment comes from your BOLTIV wallet, and the purchase is saved in your transaction history.

To buy data, choose the network and BOLTIV loads the data plans available for it. Select a plan, confirm the number and pay from your wallet. Buy Again lets you repeat a past purchase with fewer steps, and AutoPay or Scheduled Payments can buy airtime and data for you on a date you choose.

Pay electricity bills and renew DStv or GOtv

Electricity bills (prepaid and postpaid)

BOLTIV supports IKEDC (Ikeja Electric), EKEDC (Eko Electric), AEDC (Abuja Electric), KEDCO (Kano Electric), PHED (Port Harcourt Electric), IBEDC (Ibadan Electric), EEDC (Enugu Electric), JED (Jos Electric), KAEDCO (Kaduna Electric), BEDC (Benin Electric) and YEDC (Yola Electric). Choose your provider and meter type, verify the meter number so you can check the customer name, then enter the amount. The minimum purchase is ₦2,000.

Cable TV (DStv and GOtv)

Enter your smartcard or IUC number and verify it. Then choose a plan, for example DStv Compact, DStv Confam, DStv Yanga, GOtv Jolli or GOtv Max, and pay from your BOLTIV wallet. Plans and prices can change, so BOLTIV shows the current options when you buy.

What is the Maximum Payment?

The Maximum Payment is the highest amount you allow BOLTIV to charge for one automatic purchase. Prices and availability can change between the day you set up a payment and the day it runs. Your Maximum Payment is your safety limit, so BOLTIV will never automatically charge your wallet more than that amount.

How the Maximum Payment works

01
You set your limitWhen you create an AutoPay or Scheduled Payment you enter your Maximum Payment. It must be at least the current price, and the highest limit allowed is ₦1,000,000.
02
BOLTIV suggests a sensible limitFor airtime, the suggested limit is the same as the amount. For data and cable TV, it is about 10% above today's price, rounded up to the next ₦50, so a small price change does not stop your purchase.
03
BOLTIV checks before every paymentWhen the payment is due, BOLTIV checks the current price and availability of your chosen service.
04
Price within your limit: it goes aheadIf the price is at or below your Maximum Payment, the purchase is made from your wallet as planned.
05
Price above your limit: you are protectedBOLTIV does not charge your wallet and notifies you. You can approve the new price, edit the payment or cancel it.
If this happensWhat BOLTIV does
The price is the same or lower than your Maximum PaymentThe purchase goes ahead and is paid from your wallet.
The price is above your Maximum PaymentYour wallet is not charged and you are notified. For an AutoPay, tap Approve new price to raise your limit. For a Scheduled Payment, tap Approve and pay now to raise the limit and pay straight away.
A temporary network or provider issue stops the purchaseBOLTIV may retry under the AutoPay rules. Your Maximum Payment still applies to any retry.
Your wallet balance is too low (Scheduled Payments)BOLTIV tries once more later the same day. If it still cannot be paid, it is marked as failed and you are notified. You can then reschedule it or approve a price and pay straight away.
Example. You set up a monthly AutoPay for a ₦1,000 data plan with a Maximum Payment of ₦1,100, which is BOLTIV's suggested limit. If the plan costs ₦1,000 or ₦1,100 on the due date, it is bought. If the provider raises the price to ₦1,200, the AutoPay is skipped, your wallet is not charged and you receive a notification. You can then approve the new price or keep your original limit.

Tips for choosing your Maximum Payment

  • For airtime, set the Maximum Payment equal to the amount you want to buy.
  • For data and cable TV, leave a small cushion above today's price, as BOLTIV suggests, so a minor price change does not skip your purchase.
  • Keep enough money in your wallet. Your funds stay in your wallet until the payment is due.
  • Use a Scheduled Payment for a single future purchase and AutoPay for something you buy again and again.

BOLTIV CARD

Create a USD virtual card in Nigeria with the BOLTIV Card

The BOLTIV Card is a contactless USD virtual card you create inside your BOLTIV account. You fund it by paying the exact naira amount by bank transfer, and you can use it for online payments on websites and apps that accept Visa or Mastercard. Because it is contactless, it can be added to Apple Pay or Google Pay where your phone and country support it.

Card creation needs a one-time identity verification. Your card number and CVV stay hidden until you confirm your Transaction PIN, and you can freeze or unfreeze the card at any time.

BOLTIV FOR AGENTS

Become a BOLTIV Agent and resell airtime, data, electricity and cable TV

A BOLTIV Agent buys supported services at discounted BOLTIV prices, sets their own customer prices and keeps the difference as profit. Becoming an Agent upgrades the BOLTIV account you already have, with no second signup or wallet.

To qualify you need an active BOLTIV account, at least ₦10,000 in your wallet as working capital (it is not a fee), and a verified funding account set up with your NIN or BVN. BOLTIV does not promise guaranteed income. Your profit depends on the prices you set and the number of transactions you complete.

One BOLTIV wallet, secure payments and clear records

You fund your BOLTIV wallet in naira by bank transfer to your dedicated funding account. A BVN or NIN is used to set up that account, and your wallet is credited automatically once the transfer is confirmed. Every purchase and funding is recorded in your transaction history, and you can open a receipt for each transaction.

Your account uses authentication and a Transaction PIN for supported actions, so you stay in control of your money.

BOLTIV frequently asked questions

Quick answers about BOLTIV services, AutoPay, Scheduled Payments, the BOLTIV Card and the Agent programme.

What is BOLTIV?

BOLTIV is a Nigerian digital services and payments platform. With one BOLTIV account you can buy airtime and data, pay electricity bills, renew DStv and GOtv, buy WAEC, NECO and NABTEB exam PINs, send bulk airtime and bulk SMS, top up phones abroad, create a USD virtual card, and automate payments with AutoPay and Scheduled Payments.

Which networks does BOLTIV support for airtime and data?

BOLTIV supports MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile for airtime and data. You choose the network, enter the phone number and pay from your BOLTIV wallet.

Can I pay my electricity bill on BOLTIV?

Yes. You can pay prepaid or postpaid electricity bills for IKEDC (Ikeja), EKEDC (Eko), AEDC (Abuja), KEDCO (Kano), PHED (Port Harcourt), IBEDC (Ibadan), EEDC (Enugu), JED (Jos), KAEDCO (Kaduna), BEDC (Benin) and YEDC (Yola). Verify your meter number before you pay. The minimum purchase is ₦2,000.

Can I renew DStv and GOtv on BOLTIV?

Yes. Enter your smartcard or IUC number, verify it, choose a DStv or GOtv plan and pay from your BOLTIV wallet.

Can I buy WAEC, NECO or NABTEB exam PINs?

Yes. Exam PINs for WAEC, NECO and NABTEB are available from your BOLTIV wallet. Choose the exam and the number of PINs you need.

Can I send bulk airtime and bulk SMS?

Yes. Bulk Airtime recharges up to 50 numbers at once, and you pay only for the numbers that go through. Bulk SMS sends to one or thousands of recipients using your own approved sender ID.

What is AutoPay on BOLTIV?

AutoPay automatically buys your chosen Airtime, Data or Cable TV service on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule, using your BOLTIV wallet. Each run happens at 7:00 AM. You can edit, pause, resume or cancel an AutoPay at any time.

What is a Scheduled Payment, and how is it different from AutoPay?

A Scheduled Payment buys Airtime, Data or Cable TV once, on a date and time you choose (Nigeria time). It does not repeat. AutoPay repeats the same purchase on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule.

What is the Maximum Payment on AutoPay and Scheduled Payments?

The Maximum Payment is the highest amount you allow BOLTIV to charge for one automatic purchase. Before every automatic payment, BOLTIV checks the current price. If the price is within your Maximum Payment, the purchase goes ahead. If it is above, BOLTIV does not charge your wallet and notifies you.

What happens if the price goes above my Maximum Payment?

BOLTIV skips the purchase, does not charge your wallet and sends you a notification. You can then approve the new price. For an AutoPay, approving raises your limit. For a Scheduled Payment, approving raises the limit and pays straight away. You can also edit or cancel it.

How do I fund my BOLTIV wallet?

You fund your wallet by bank transfer to your dedicated funding account. A BVN or NIN is used to set up that account. Your wallet is credited automatically after the transfer is confirmed.

What is the BOLTIV Card?

The BOLTIV Card is a contactless USD virtual card you create inside your BOLTIV account and fund by bank transfer in naira. You can use it for online payments wherever Visa or Mastercard is accepted. Card issuance needs a one-time identity verification.

How do I become a BOLTIV Agent?

Sign in to your BOLTIV account, choose Become an Agent from your profile, keep at least ₦10,000 in your wallet as working capital, and complete verification of your funding account with your NIN or BVN. BOLTIV does not promise guaranteed income.

Is BOLTIV secure?

BOLTIV includes account authentication and transaction security controls, including a Transaction PIN for supported account actions such as creating an AutoPay or a Scheduled Payment.
