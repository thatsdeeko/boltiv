Frequently asked questions

How much does BOLTIV Link cost?

₦3,000 for 100 days of access, paid from your BOLTIV wallet. The price and period are shown live on this page. Everything is included: the website, orders, bookings, customer list, analytics and marketing tools. There is no setup fee.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. You pick a design, type in your details, upload photos and publish. Everything is built for phones, so you can manage your whole business from one.

Can customers order from my website?

Yes. Customers add products to a cart, choose pickup or delivery, apply a promo code and place an order. You get a notification, see the order in your inbox, confirm it and update its status. Customers can also send you the order on WhatsApp.

Can customers pay on the website?

Not yet. Orders and bookings are saved on your dashboard and you arrange payment directly with the customer, by transfer, cash or on delivery. You can mark orders as paid so your records stay tidy.

How do bookings work?

You set the days and hours you work, how long each slot is, and any holidays. Customers pick a service, a date and a free time. Taken times disappear automatically, so nobody is double-booked. You can confirm bookings yourself or let them confirm instantly.

How many photos can each product have?

Up to 8. You can upload several at once, reorder them, choose the main photo and replace any of them. On your website customers see a gallery with thumbnails, swipe on their phone and tap to view each photo full size, without cropping.

What happens if my plan expires?

Your website goes offline and visitors see a short unavailable page. Your content, photos, orders, bookings and customers stay safe. Renew and your website is back straight away. Your name stays reserved for you.

Who owns my customer information?

You do. Customers who order, book or send an enquiry are saved to your customer list. You can add notes and tags, and export the list at any time. Only customers who tick the offers box appear in your marketing list.
