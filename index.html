Questions, answered.

Quick answers about how BOLTIV works.

What is BOLTIV?

BOLTIV is a business technology ecosystem. It brings a business website, business management tools, payments, a USD virtual card, smart payment features and everyday digital services together under one account and one wallet.

Who is BOLTIV for?

Individuals who want simple everyday payments, entrepreneurs starting out, SMEs that need to organise sales, stock and teams, and established businesses that need control across branches.

Do I need to use every product?

No. Each product works on its own. Start with the one you need, and add others later without opening a new account.

How does the BOLTIV wallet work?

You fund your wallet with an available payment method and use the balance for supported purchases and subscriptions across BOLTIV, including Business Manager and BOLTIV Link.

Can I earn with BOLTIV?

Yes. The BOLTIV Agent programme lets you buy airtime, data and bill payments at discounted prices, set your own customer prices and keep the profit.

How do I get started?

Create a BOLTIV account, verify your email, fund your wallet and choose the product you want. You can sign in any time to add more.

Is BOLTIV secure?

BOLTIV uses account authentication, email verification, a transaction PIN for supported actions and login history. Business Manager adds role-based access and audit logs.

What if I need help?

Visit the contact page and the team will help with your account or services.
