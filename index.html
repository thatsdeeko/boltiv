FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is BOLTIV?

BOLTIV is a digital services and payments platform that brings everyday services, wallet payments, smart payment tools and rewards into one account.

What services can I use on BOLTIV?

BOLTIV currently supports Airtime, Data, Electricity, Cable TV and Exam PINs, with additional services planned as the platform grows.

How does the BOLTIV Wallet work?

Fund your wallet using an available payment method, then use your available balance for supported services and review your activity from your account.

What are BOLTIV Smart Features?

Smart Features include AutoPay, Scheduled Payments, Favorites, Buy Again, Spending Insights and Transaction Alerts.

What is BOLTIV Agent?

BOLTIV Agent is the business side of BOLTIV for people who want to resell supported digital services, access Agent pricing, set customer prices and track business performance.

Is BOLTIV secure?

BOLTIV uses account authentication and transaction security controls, including transaction PIN protection for supported account actions.
