Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about using BOLTIV.

What is BOLTIV?

BOLTIV is a digital services and payments platform that brings everyday services such as airtime, data, electricity and cable TV into one account.

How do I get started?

Create a BOLTIV account, complete the required account setup and then sign in to access the services available to you.

How does the BOLTIV wallet work?

You can fund your wallet using an available payment method and use your available balance for supported services and transactions.

Can I see my transactions?

Yes. Your account includes transaction activity and history so you can review purchases and wallet funding.

Is BOLTIV secure?

BOLTIV includes account authentication and transaction security controls, including a transaction PIN for supported account actions.

What if I need help?

Visit the support or contact section to get assistance with your BOLTIV account or services.
