Questions

How do I fund my wallet?

Sign in, tap Fund Wallet and pay with an available payment method. The balance is ready to spend right away.

How does cashback work?

Data purchases earn 1% back on ₦100 to ₦1,000 and 2% back on ₦1,000 to ₦10,000. Above that, cashback is capped at ₦200 per purchase. Bonus balance expires after 90 days.

Is my money safe?

Every purchase needs your transaction PIN, and every transaction gets a reference you can find in your history.

Can I earn by selling BOLTIV services?

Yes. Upgrade your account to an Agent, buy at agent prices, set your own prices and keep the difference.
