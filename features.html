AutoPay

For supported services, AutoPay is designed for purchases you make repeatedly and want BOLTIV to handle automatically.

What AutoPay does

Instead of manually starting the same supported Airtime, Data or Cable TV purchase every time, you can create an AutoPay arrangement and let BOLTIV handle the repeat purchase according to the details you set.

  • Choose a supported service and the details you want to use.
  • Set up the AutoPay arrangement from your BOLTIV account.
  • Let BOLTIV run the supported purchase automatically when it is due.
  • Review your AutoPay activity through your account notifications.

Why it helps

AutoPay is useful for recurring needs where remembering to make the same purchase can become inconvenient. It reduces repetitive steps and helps you keep routine supported purchases organized.

Scheduled Payments

Not ready to pay now? Schedule a supported purchase for a date and time you choose.

How scheduling works

Scheduled Payments lets you set up a supported Airtime, Data or Cable TV purchase to run once in the future. You choose the service details and the date and time you want the transaction to be attempted.

  • Select a supported service.
  • Enter the purchase details.
  • Choose your future date and time.
  • Review the scheduled transaction in BOLTIV.

Why it helps

Scheduling is useful when you already know when you want a purchase to happen but do not want to rely on remembering it later. It gives you more control over when supported transactions are initiated.

Favorites

Save the details you use frequently and make future purchases faster.

What you can save

BOLTIV Favorites is designed around frequently used purchase details. You can save numbers and smartcards you regularly buy for, making them easier to select the next time you need the same service.

  • Keep frequently used details available inside your BOLTIV account.
  • Give saved details recognizable names where supported.
  • Select a saved favorite instead of typing the same information again.
  • Manage your saved favorites as your needs change.

Why it helps

Favorites are especially useful when you regularly purchase services for the same phone number, smartcard or recipient. Less typing means fewer repetitive steps and a quicker route to checkout.

Buy Again

Repeat successful purchases without rebuilding the transaction from scratch.

How it works

BOLTIV can surface services you have successfully purchased before so you can repeat them quickly. Instead of starting over, choose a previous purchase and use the available quick-repeat action.

  • Use your previous successful purchases as a shortcut.
  • Choose the service you want to repeat.
  • Review the transaction details before confirming.
  • Complete the purchase through the normal BOLTIV flow.

Why it helps

If you frequently buy the same type of service, Buy Again reduces unnecessary navigation and re-entry while keeping the final transaction under your control.

Spending Insights

Turn your transaction activity into information you can actually use.

What you can see

Spending Insights gives you a clearer view of your BOLTIV activity by showing how much you spend each month and which services contribute to that spending.

  • Review your monthly spending.
  • Understand which services you use most.
  • Spot patterns in your everyday payment activity.
  • Use the information to make more informed decisions about future purchases.

Why it helps

A transaction history tells you what happened. Spending Insights helps you step back and understand the pattern behind those transactions, giving you a simpler way to monitor everyday service spending.

Transaction Alerts

Stay informed about important activity instead of having to constantly check your account.

What alerts can cover

BOLTIV notifications help surface important account and service activity, including transaction-related updates, AutoPay runs and data plans that are about to expire.

  • Review relevant account notifications.
  • See updates connected to AutoPay activity.
  • Receive useful reminders about supported service activity.
  • Keep important updates in one place inside BOLTIV.

Why it helps

Notifications reduce the need to repeatedly check every part of your account. They help bring important events to your attention so you can act when necessary.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about BOLTIV Smart Features.

Do I need to log in to read about Smart Features?

No. This page is public and can be viewed without logging in. You only need a BOLTIV account when you want to use the Smart Features themselves.

Which Smart Features does BOLTIV currently offer?

The current Smart Features page includes AutoPay, Scheduled Payments, Favorites, Buy Again, Spending Insights and Transaction Alerts.

Does AutoPay support every BOLTIV service?

No. AutoPay supports the services enabled for it in BOLTIV. The current Smart Features experience identifies Airtime, Data and Cable TV as supported AutoPay purchase types.

Can I schedule a payment for any service?

Scheduled Payments are available for supported services. The current BOLTIV experience supports Airtime, Data and Cable TV scheduling.

What is the difference between Favorites and Buy Again?

Favorites lets you save frequently used purchase details for quick access. Buy Again lets you repeat a service you have successfully purchased before.

Where can I see my spending information?

Spending Insights are available inside your signed-in BOLTIV experience and show monthly spending and service-level activity.

Are Smart Features separate from my BOLTIV account?

No. They are built into the BOLTIV experience and work alongside your existing services, wallet and transaction activity.
