About the BOLTIV Card

How it works
  1. Verify your identity. A one-time check using your BVN, a valid government ID and two photos, so we can issue a card in your name.
  2. Create your card. Choose Visa or Mastercard and the amount in US dollars to load. The naira equivalent is deducted from your BOLTIV wallet.
  3. Fund it whenever you need. Top up instantly from your wallet.
  4. Spend online. Use it wherever Visa or Mastercard is accepted online in USD. You can freeze or unfreeze it at any time.
Pricing
  • You pay the card amount plus a one-time card creation fee, converted to naira at the BOLTIV rate shown before you confirm. The exact naira total is always displayed first.
  • Funding your card carries no additional card fee.
Important to know
  • Each customer can hold one card.
  • The card is intended for online payments, mainly with US merchants. Some categories, including money transfer, cryptocurrency, betting and gambling, dating and adult services, are not supported and may be declined.
  • Payments declined for insufficient funds may attract a fee, and a card is closed after repeated insufficient-funds declines. Please keep your card funded.
  • Payments in a currency other than USD may attract additional charges.
Security
  • Your card number and CVV are shown only after you confirm your Transaction PIN, are hidden automatically after 30 seconds, and are never stored by BOLTIV.
  • If a card request cannot be completed, the amount is returned to your wallet automatically.
